## Full description

Find parking before you arrive with BAY, a parking finder built to help drivers compare nearby options and understand what the available source information says.

Search a destination or use your current location, then explore mapped parking in map or list view. BAY brings together parking areas, available street-parking information, source-listed restrictions, published prices and freshness-aware availability in one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH BAY

• Search a destination or start from your current location  
• Find parking near you with 500 m, 1 km, 2 km and 5 km search radiuses  
• Switch between a visual map and a detailed parking list  
• Open parking details before requesting directions  
• Review source-listed access conditions, restrictions, capacity and prices where published  
• See fresh vacancy or occupancy readings where BAY has a compatible current data feed  
• Save home and work for quicker future searches  
• Learn how common parking signs work in the in-app guide  
• Report an incorrect location, restriction, price or navigation issue for review

BUILT FOR HONEST PARKING INFORMATION

BAY distinguishes a mapped parking location from a live availability reading. A marker does not guarantee that a space will be free when you arrive. Availability labels are shown only when a supported source has supplied a sufficiently recent reading.

Prices, restrictions, opening hours and access conditions vary by location and source. When a machine-readable rate is unavailable, BAY prompts the driver to check the current rate at the meter or entrance instead of estimating one. Always check the physical parking sign, road markings and current operator terms before parking.

MELBOURNE PARKING AND BEYOND

BAY provides detailed parking discovery in Melbourne where supported council, operator and map data is available. Destination search can also return mapped parking in other supported locations, but the level of detail, live availability and pricing varies by place and source.

HOW BAY WORKS

1. Search where you are going.  
2. Compare nearby parking information.  
3. Open a result and check the source details.  
4. Choose a suitable location and open directions.

BAY is designed for planning before or after you stop—not for interaction while driving.

Download BAY and know more before you arrive.

Important: physical signs, road markings, local regulations and parking-operator terms remain authoritative. BAY cannot guarantee that a mapped place is legal, accessible or available when you arrive.

## Detailed release notes

- Refined nearby parking map and list experience.
- Added Nearest, Cheapest and Available sorting with clear 30 min to all-day stay choices.
- Default nearby search starts at 500 m with parking areas included.
- Shows a total for the selected stay when a source publishes a compatible price.
- Clearer separation between mapped places and fresh live availability readings.
- Stale readings are hidden and replaced with the neutral “Check on arrival” status.
- Green markers mean live openings, red means confirmed full, and BAY blue marks parking where availability should be checked on arrival.
- Source-listed prices and restrictions are shown when supplied; otherwise BAY directs drivers to check the current rate at the location.
- Added the broader mapped Melbourne off-street catalogue to the main map and results.
- Updated the app icon to the white-and-BAY-blue brand treatment.
- Added a structured “Report incorrect parking information” flow for location, restriction, price and navigation issues.
- Improved saved-place, activity and parking-guide experiences.
- Reliability, responsive-layout and production-build improvements.
