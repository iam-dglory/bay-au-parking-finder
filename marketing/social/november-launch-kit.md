## Ten short-video scripts

1. **Google Maps got me here. Now what?** Arrive near a busy destination; freeze frame before circling. Open BAY, search the destination, compare options, check details. End: “Choose before the final turn.”
2. **The marker honesty test.** Show a marker, then overlay: “Mapped ≠ guaranteed available.” Open the freshness explanation. End: “BAY tells you what the source actually knows.”
3. **One price, two truths.** Compare a result with a published tariff and one with no supplied price. End: “No estimate is better than a made-up number.”
4. **500 metres first.** Search Melbourne, show the default radius, then widen it. End: “Start close. Expand only when you need to.”
5. **Can I park here?** Hold on a common 2P sign, open the BAY guide, explain time limit versus payment. End: “Then read every plate on arrival.”
6. **The ten-second parking check.** Search → List → open detail → source. Use an actual timer; do not make time-saving claims beyond the recorded clip.
7. **Parking data humbled us.** Show a missing price, stale sensor and report flow. End: “Good parking software must show uncertainty.”
8. **Fix the map with us.** Open a wrong result, choose the report category, explain that reports are reviewed before data changes.
9. **Map or list?** Split screen with the same real results. Ask viewers which they use; CTA to test both.
10. **Marketplace gate, not hype.** Flash moderation, pricing, payment, refund and legal checks. End: “Private-space booking launches only when the whole chain is verified.”
