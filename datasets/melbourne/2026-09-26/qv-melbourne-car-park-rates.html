If you’re looking for cheap or discounted parking in the heart of Melbourne, look no further. These park-and-shop discount offers make the QV Car Park the best place to start exploring Melbourne’s famous dining, shopping, arts and sporting culture.

1 Hour Free*

2 Hours Free*

When you spend $60+ at Woolworths, Big W, Dan Murphy’s, Domayne & Harvey Norman. Request a discount parking ticket at time of purchase.

Woolworths, Big W and Dan Murphy’s receipts are automatically valid for pay station or exit gate.
 

 

STAYING LONGER?

 

$23 All Day Weekday Parking

When you spend $1 or more at any QV store.

Request a parking discount ticket at time of purchase. 

 

How to apply your parking discount

Pay station: Visit the pay stations located on Level B1 or B2 and insert your original ticket, followed by the discount ticket (or receipt for the free parking offer) and your parking will be automatically discounted.

Exit gate: Scan your ticket and receipt at the car park exit gate where your discount will be automatically applied.
 

*Total spend of $30 or $60 must be from one store only from the list of applicable QV Melbourne stores listed above. Big W, Woolworths and Dan Murphy’s discounts will be printed automatically on your transaction receipt, however you need to collect a discount ticket from Harvey Norman or Domayne at time of purchase. Wish cards, Prepaid MasterCard/VISA cards, Big W Gift cards, Woolworths/Woolworths Liquor Gift cards and cash outs are not applicable.

 

Enjoy everything that Melbourne has to offer when you choose to park at QV. Close to theatres, sporting venues, universities, museums - and of course great shopping - QV’s dedicated accessible parking options are your starting point for every city visit.

● Accessible parking spaces on all levels
● Parents’ parking on Level B1
● Lift and travelator access from all levels to QV Melbourne, as well as direct access to Lonsdale Street, Little Lonsdale Street, Swanston Street and Russell Street. 

To book or enquire about permanent parking opportunities at QV, email staff.qv@pointparking.com.au. 

Is free parking available?

Get 1-hour free parking when you spend $30+, or 2 hours free parking when you spend $60+ at Woolworths, Big W, Dan Murphy’s, Domayne & Harvey Norman. Read more about these parking offers, including terms and conditions under Parking Offers.

 

What payment methods are accepted?

QV Car Park is cashless. Accepted payment methods include credit and debit cards.

Gift cards are not accepted.

 

Where are the disabled car parking spaces located at QV Melbourne?

There are disabled car parking spaces located near the precinct entrances on each level of the QV Melbourne Car Park.

 

Is there lift or escalator access from the QV Car Park?

You can exit the QV Car Park by lift or escalator from B1, B2 or B3 levels of the QV Melbourne Car Park.

 

Is there pram parking at QV?

Yes, there are designated pram car parking spaces located on B1 of the QV Melbourne Car Park.

 

Is long-term parking available at QV Melbourne?

Additional fees will apply for long-term parking. To enquire about long-term parking, email staff.qv@pointparking.com.au.

 

What is the maximum vehicle height to enter the car park?

There is a maximum height clearance of 2.1m to enter the QV Melbourne Car Park.

 

What are the QV Car Park opening hours?

The QV Melbourne Car Park is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

 

Are there charging stations available for electric cars?

No, this is not currently available at QV Melbourne.

 

What should I do if I lose my car?

Visit the car park office located on B1, near the escalators and Star Car Wash.

 

Is staff car parking available?

Car parking at QV Melbourne is reserved for customers only.
