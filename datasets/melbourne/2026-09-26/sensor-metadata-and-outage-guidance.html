On-street Parking Bay Sensors

This dataset contains information from in-ground car parking bay sensors across the city with status indicating if a car is present or not present, the spatial coordinate of the sensor, and the Kerbside id.

Additional information:
Further information about City of Melbourne's parking sensors can be found here.


Outages
This dataset is updated through network relays and can be disrupted causing delays in data updates. Users of this data are encouraged to monitor the dataset's last updated timestamp (Lastupdated column) to check for delays in the data being updated.
