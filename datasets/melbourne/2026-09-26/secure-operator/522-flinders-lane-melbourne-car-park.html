522 Flinders Lane, Melbourne Car Park

522 Flinders Lane, Melbourne VIC 3000
2.00m

Park in the heart of Melbourne at 522 Flinders Lane, ideally located in the CBD’s vibrant business and dining precinct. Perfect Melbourne parking for workdays, meetings, or exploring the city, this conveniently positioned car park puts you just moments from offices, cafes, and laneway attractions. Enjoy easy, reliable parking so you can focus on making the most of your time in Melbourne.

This car park is best for:

  • Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre Parking
  • MCEC Car Parking
  • Melbourne CBD Parking
  • Collins Street Parking
  • Docklands Parking
  • Flinders Street Parking

Directions

How to Pay for your Parking:

1. Tap in, Tap out
Simply drive in and tap your credit card or phone. Your vehicle will be automatically registered on entry. When leaving, tap out using the same card or device to pay.

2. Pre‑Purchase
When you pre‑pay, you’ll receive a PIN or QR code for entry and exit. This will be sent via email and is also available in the Account Purchases section when logged in on your device. Learn more here.

3. Pay at ticket machine
Pull a ticket on entry, then use the pay machines inside the car park with your card or mobile phone before returning to your car.
Keep your ticket handy, you’ll need it to exit.

4. Monthly Parking
For Monthly Subscription enquiries, please call 1300 048 524 between 7am–7pm AEST, Monday to Friday.
