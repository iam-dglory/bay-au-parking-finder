Car Park Access

Home › Parking › Car Park Access

Shopping at Queen Vic Market has never been easier, with plenty of parking available for customers across both our Multi-level Undercover Car Park and Open Air Car Park. View our parking rates.

Open Air Car Park:

  • Entry to the car park is accessible via Queen Street. Customers can exit onto Peel Street, Queen Street or Franklin Street.

Multi-level Undercover Car Park:

  •  Located directly opposite the Open Air Car Park entry, access is available via Queen Street.
