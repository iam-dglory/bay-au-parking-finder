Vehicle Entry Restrictions

Entry restrictions apply to different vehicle classes at different times within Hyderabad city limits. Refer to the official G.O. for complete details.

🚛
Heavy Goods Vehicles
Restricted entry into city core during peak hours. Check designated routes.
🚌
Public Transport Buses
Designated routes and bus bays applicable throughout the city.
🏍️
Two Wheelers
Flyover and expressway restrictions apply at designated points.
🚐
School Vehicles
Must comply with GHMC NOC requirements and prescribed timing norms.
