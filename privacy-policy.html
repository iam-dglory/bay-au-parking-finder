Bay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Bay helps people find mapped parking places and understand available parking information. This policy covers the Bay app, website, early-access waitlist and related feedback forms. “Bay”, “we” and “us” refer to the Bay app team. For privacy questions, contact gopikaaravindoffl@gmail.com.

At a glance

You can search for parking without joining the waitlist. If you use your location, Bay uses it to find nearby places and records search coordinates with an anonymous app identifier. Sign photos you upload have public URLs, even while a sign report is pending review. Waitlist email addresses are kept out of the public parking map.

1. About Bay

Bay combines public parking datasets, available live sensor readings, parking-area information and community sign submissions. Coverage, prices, restrictions and live availability vary by location and source. Using the parking map creates an anonymous app session; it does not require you to provide a name or email address.

2. Information We Collect

Location information

If you allow location access, we receive your device’s coordinates to find nearby parking and show your position. If you search manually, we use the place or coordinates you choose instead. For searches that return results, Bay stores the search latitude and longitude, radius, result count, time and anonymous app user ID. A selected sign pin and address are stored when you submit a sign.

Waitlist information

The current early-access form asks only for an email address, Android or iPhone choice, and the country where you drive most (Australia, India or the United States). We also store signup time and form/consent version. Earlier waitlist forms may have collected a name, suburb, driving frequency or whether the person drives in Melbourne. We keep these historical responses private; we do not infer a country for an older signup.

Parking-sign photographs and submissions

When you add a sign, we collect its photo, pinned location, address, rules, notes you enter, submission time and anonymous app user ID. A photo may contain information visible in the scene or embedded in the image file.

Feedback

In-app feedback can include your message, optional rating, page context, submission time and anonymous app user ID. The separate survey can include ratings, written answers and an optional tester number. Please do not put personal or sensitive information in free-text answers unless needed for your request.

App activity and technical information

Supabase creates an anonymous authentication session so Bay can link contributions and activity to a user ID. Bay records certain parking searches and when you request directions to a sign, including the referenced place and time. Your browser, app platform and service providers may also process technical information such as IP address, request logs and device/browser details needed to deliver and secure the service.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to show nearby parking and rules; display available sensor readings; process and review sign submissions; remember your contributions and directions activity; organise early-access invitations and launch updates; respond to feedback and privacy requests; measure use and improve accuracy; and protect Bay from misuse. Waitlist details are used for Bay early-access and launch communications, not added to public parking datasets.

4. Location Data

Location permission is optional for finding parking: you can choose a city or place manually. When you grant permission, Bay uses location while you use the nearby-parking feature, including updates as your position changes. Search coordinates can be stored with your anonymous app ID as described above; they are not merely processed on your device. Coordinates may be sent to Supabase to retrieve nearby results and to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to turn a location into an address. You can revoke location permission in your device or browser settings, though nearby results may then require a manual search.

5. Photographs and User Submissions

You choose a photo using your camera or photo library when submitting a parking sign. Sign details are reviewed before a new report appears on the shared map. The uploaded photo is stored in a publicly readable Supabase storage bucket and has a public URL as soon as it is uploaded, even if the related report is pending or its submission later fails. Do not upload faces, number plates, private documents or other personal information. Approved sign details and their photos may be visible to other users. Contact us to request removal of a report or photo; include its link or approximate location so we can find it.

Older beta versions accepted manual occupied/vacant observations. The current app no longer offers that reporting control. Historical observations may remain in a publicly readable legacy table, including the spot, status, submission time and anonymous app user ID, until removed under our retention process.

6. Third-Party Services / Supabase

  • Supabase provides anonymous authentication, the parking database, sign-photo storage, waitlist storage and feedback storage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Bay website and may receive ordinary website request information.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers and Nominatim provide map tiles and place search/reverse geocoding. Relevant map requests, search terms or coordinates, and network information are sent to those services.
  • City of Melbourne may receive requests for published sensor data when the Melbourne live layer loads.
  • Google Maps receives a destination when you choose “Get directions” and open its service.

These services may have their own privacy practices. Bay also displays parking information from public authorities and other map or operator sources; displaying their data does not mean they receive your waitlist email.

7. Data Sharing

We share information with the service providers above as needed to operate Bay. Approved sign submissions and their photos can be shown publicly, and sign-photo URLs can be accessed directly. Waitlist entries, search-event records, feedback and survey answers are not exposed through Bay’s public data endpoints. We do not sell personal information or provide it to advertisers. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

8. Data Retention

We keep waitlist entries while managing early access and launch communications, and keep app activity, feedback and submissions while needed to operate, audit and improve Bay. We have not set a universal automatic deletion date for these records. We review removal requests and delete data we can identify when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements and provider or operational backups. A sign photo may remain in public storage until it is separately removed, even if a pending database submission failed.

9. Data Security

Bay uses encrypted network connections and Supabase access controls for non-public database records. Access is limited by the role of the user or service. No system can guarantee perfect security, and public sign photos should be treated as public before you upload them. Please contact us if you believe a submission or your information has been exposed incorrectly.

10. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • Use manual place search instead of granting device-location access; revoke permission later in device or browser settings.
  • Do not join the waitlist or submit a sign or feedback if you do not want to provide that information.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your waitlist entry, feedback, sign report, photo or associated anonymous app data by emailing gopikaaravindoffl@gmail.com. For a waitlist request, write from the signup email if possible. For app data, include the sign link or location and any details that help us locate your anonymous record. We may need to verify that a request relates to you before making changes.

Deleting the app or clearing browser data does not automatically delete records already sent to Bay. We will explain if we cannot identify a record or must retain specific information for a lawful reason. If you have a privacy complaint, email us with “Privacy complaint” in the subject; we will review it and respond. Australian users may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner if their concern remains unresolved.

11. Children’s Privacy

Bay is intended for people looking for parking and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

12. International Data Processing

Bay is available to people in Australia, India and the United States. Supabase, GitHub and the other services listed above may process information outside your country, including in the United States. The location of processing can vary by provider and service. Where required, we will take steps to handle cross-border information in accordance with applicable privacy law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Bay’s features or data practices change. We will publish the revised text here and change the date at the top. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by other appropriate means before they take effect.

14. Contact Us

For privacy questions, access, correction, deletion or complaints, contact the Bay app team at gopikaaravindoffl@gmail.com. Tell us what you need and provide enough detail to locate the relevant record, without sending passwords or unrelated sensitive documents.

15. Parking-information disclaimer

Parking data, signs, opening hours, prices and sensor readings can be incomplete, delayed or change without notice. A mapped space or vacant reading does not guarantee that parking is permitted or available when you arrive. Check the physical sign, road markings and current operator terms before parking. This parking-information notice is separate from Bay’s privacy practices.
