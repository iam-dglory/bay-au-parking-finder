Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Bay ("we", "us") helps people find parking — free public spots, and rules reported by the community — in Australia and worldwide. This page explains what information Bay collects, why, and how you can contact us about it.

What Bay does not collect

Bay does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. There is no account sign-up. Bay uses anonymous authentication: your device is given a random, anonymous identifier so your reports and activity are linked to that identifier only — never to your real identity.

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Location (GPS, or the city/suburb you search for)To show parking spots and rules near you. Location is used on your device to query nearby results and is not stored against your identity beyond what's needed to serve that request.
Parking signs and spots you report (address, pin location, sign rules, notes, and a photo of the sign)To build the shared, public map of parking availability that every user relies on. A photo is required for every report and is reviewed before the report is shown to anyone else, to keep the map accurate and free of spam. The photo is publicly visible once approved, the same as the rest of the report.
Occupancy reports ("mark occupied/free") and your device location at the moment you submit oneTo show other nearby users a live, time-limited signal about a spot — these expire automatically (occupancy reports become "unknown" after 30 minutes). Your location at submission time is checked against the spot's location to confirm you're actually near it, but is not stored — only the fact that the check passed.
Anonymous device identifierTo let you see your own reports and history ("My activity"), and to attribute contributions without collecting personal information.

Who we share it with

Bay is built on a small number of infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf, strictly to run the app:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and anonymous authentication.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — provides map tiles and location search. Search queries you type (e.g. a city or suburb name) are sent to this service to find matching places.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Data you contribute publicly

Parking signs (once approved), their photos, and occupancy signals are shared with everyone using Bay — that's how a crowdsourced parking directory works. Please don't include personal information (yours or anyone else's, including number plates or faces) in a sign photo, note, or description you submit.

Your choices

  • Location access is optional for finding parking — you can search for a city manually instead of sharing your device's location. It is required to submit an occupancy report, since that's how we confirm you're near the spot.
  • Camera access is required to report a new parking sign, since a photo is mandatory for every report.
  • Because Bay doesn't collect identifying information, there's no traditional account to delete. If you'd like a specific report removed, contact us below with the address or approximate location and we'll remove it.

How reports are reviewed

A new parking sign report isn't shown to other users right away. It's checked against its photo first, to confirm it shows a real, current sign matching what was reported. Until that check is done, only you can see it (marked "Pending review" in My Activity); if it's rejected, we'll leave it visible to you as rejected rather than silently discard it.

Children's privacy

Bay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of Bay after a change means you accept the updated policy.
