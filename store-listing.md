## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Stop circling the block. Bay shows you every parking sign, spot, and rule near you — and tells you, right now, whether it's actually free, paid, or off-limits.

WHAT BAY DOES
• Find nearby parking instantly — free, time-limited, paid meters, permit zones, and clearways, all colour-coded on the map.
• Understand the actual rule in plain English — "Free until 6pm", "Paid $6/hr", "Permit holders only" — no more squinting at confusing signage.
• Localised to how your country actually signs parking. Australia's 2P/permit/clearway system looks completely different from India's informal, high-leeway street parking, or the US/UK's metered and permit conventions — Bay adapts the reporting form to match, automatically, based on where you are.
• Report a sign in seconds — drop a pin, take a photo of the actual sign, and pick what it says in plain language. Every report is checked against its photo before it goes live, so the map stays trustworthy.
• See live occupancy reports — mark a spot "occupied" or "free" when you're actually there (we check you're nearby before accepting a report); reports fade back to "unknown" after 30 minutes, and show how many people agree, so you're never trusting one stale, unverifiable tap.
• Track your own activity — see your reported signs (and whether they've been approved yet) and everywhere you've parked, in one place.
• Works worldwide — search any country, state, and city. Built and launching first in Australia, usable everywhere.

WHY BAY IS DIFFERENT
Most parking apps only know where a parking spot physically is. Bay knows the rule — and whether that rule currently applies. A spot can be legally free right now but have a car sitting in it 2 minutes ago; Bay surfaces both pieces of information so you can make a real decision, not a guess.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Bay works instantly with no sign-up — you're anonymously and privately connected the moment you open the app, so you can start reporting and searching right away.

FREE TO USE
Bay is completely free to download and use. Payment for paid parking happens the normal way, in person, once you arrive — Bay never processes payments.
